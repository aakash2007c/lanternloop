A SMALL ORIGINAL ARCADE GAME

🏮 Lantern Loop

FIREFLIES 0 / 10
TIME 60s
LIVES 3
Move with WASD / arrow keys or touch controls. Collect 10 fireflies; avoid shadow moths.
How to play

You are the tiny lantern. Each firefly collected adds one point. Touching a shadow moth costs one life and briefly protects you. Collect 10 fireflies before the 60-second timer ends to win. You have three lives.
